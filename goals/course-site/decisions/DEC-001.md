## Question and options

Which two chapters become the reference chapters that all later chapters copy (as chapters 1 and 2 did in options-explained)? Decide after reading the merged `docs/CURRICULUM.md` (TASK-002).

- **A. The first two Foundations chapters.** Simple, like options-explained. But they use little real data, so the data-widget pattern stays untested.
- **B. The first Foundations chapter + the first predictability chapter (Week 1).** One chapter sets the "from zero" voice. The other sets the real-data pattern (for example the dividend–price ratio and future returns from `shiller-monthly`). Later chapters have a model for both.
- **C. Two Week-1 chapters.** Strong data examples, but no model for the gentle Foundations voice.

Criteria: the pair should show the full template, one real-data widget, one simulated (seeded) widget, and the voice for a reader new to the subject.

## Evidence

`docs/CURRICULUM.md` (after TASK-002) and `docs/DATA.md` (after TASK-004).

## Recommendation

B. It is the only option that gives later chapters a model for both the beginner voice and a real-data widget. Name the two chapter numbers when you accept.
